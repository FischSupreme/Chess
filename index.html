How to play chess

  1. Goal: Checkmate your opponent's king. The king must be in check with no legal way to escape.
  2. White moves first. Players alternate one move at a time.
  3. Pawn: Moves one square forward (two from its starting square), captures one square diagonally forward; can promote on the far rank.
  4. Rook: Moves horizontally or vertically any number of unobstructed squares.
  5. Bishop: Moves diagonally any number of unobstructed squares.
  6. Knight: Moves in an L shape and can jump over pieces.
  7. Queen: Moves like a rook or bishop.
  8. King: Moves one square in any direction, cannot move into check. It may castle with a rook when allowed.
  9. Special moves: Castling, en passant and pawn promotion are supported.
  10. Draws: Stalemate, repetition, insufficient material, the 50-move rule or an agreed draw.
  11. Time: In timed games, running out of time usually loses, subject to insufficient mating material.

An admin-created position may depart from ordinary game rules. Competitive protection against modified clients requires a trusted chess server.
